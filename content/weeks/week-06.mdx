## Tutor feedback (AS1, team component)

_Feedback received on the group submission._

**What was done well:**

The report presents a clear and relevant business analytical question that aligns with the project requirements. The methodology is well organised and follows a logical machine learning workflow, with data exploration, preprocessing, clustering, and regression modelling explained clearly. A range of machine learning techniques were applied appropriately, and the comparison between clustering and prediction approaches shows good technical understanding. The report makes effective use of visualisations to support the analysis and communicate findings. The discussion acknowledges limitations within the dataset and demonstrates some critical reflection on the findings.

**What could be improved:**

The report spends a significant amount of space on background information and literature review; more emphasis should have been placed on analysing and interpreting the results. The business impact could have been discussed in greater depth, as the recommendations are relevant but not always linked to measurable outcomes for Airbnb. The evaluation of model performance is relatively brief, and a more critical discussion of why the models achieved limited predictive power would have strengthened it. The identified clusters are presented clearly, but the practical implications for different stakeholder groups could have been explored further. Some visualisations contain a large amount of information and could be simplified to improve clarity and accessibility.

**General comments for future work:**

This is a solid submission that demonstrates a good understanding of the machine learning process and appropriate use of analytical techniques. For future work, the feedback recommends focusing more on interpreting results and explaining their business value rather than providing extensive contextual background, and strengthening the report through more critical evaluation of model performance, discussion of alternative approaches, and clearer evidence of business impact. Overall, the work shows good technical competence and a structured approach, but stronger critical analysis and deeper business insight would be needed for a higher mark.
